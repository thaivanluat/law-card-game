How to play

Suit powers

Combat

  • Play one card, an Ace + one card, or equal-rank number combos totaling ≤10.
  • Deal damage, then resolve suit powers (unless the enemy is immune to that suit).
  • Diamonds (official): clockwise shared draw. Home rule: each player draws up to value.
  • Defend by discarding enough value to cover the enemy’s attack (after Spades).
  • Players: 2–4 co-op (hand 7 / 6 / 5). Solo: hand 8 + 2 set-aside Jesters.

Jesters

  • Co-op: play from hand to cancel immunity, skip strike, choose next player.
  • Solo: flip set-aside Jester to discard hand and refill to 8.
